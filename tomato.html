01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

A smarter way to organise your self-care.

Tomato AI is a planned Android application that combines an AI assistant with health education, beauty information and personal routine management.

The idea is to create one convenient place where users can explore skincare advice, learn about wellness topics, research supplements and organise daily habits with reminders.

Instead of searching across many different sources, users will be able to ask questions, explore useful information and build routines suited to their needs.

06 · RESPONSIBLE AI

Helpful information, responsibly presented.

Tomato AI is intended to support learning and everyday organisation. It is not designed to replace a doctor, dermatologist or other qualified healthcare professional.

Health-related information should communicate uncertainty, distinguish research evidence from traditional claims and encourage professional advice when appropriate.

Design principle: the app should help users understand information and organise routines without promising medical outcomes or encouraging users to replace prescribed treatment.
