02 / WEB DEVELOPMENT

Halinga
Restaurant
& Arcade

A responsive website developed for a family restaurant, retro arcade and entertainment venue in Estonia. The website presents the restaurant, arcade machines, events and visitor information in four languages.

Main features

Responsive Website

Multilingual Content

Information presented in Estonian, English, Russian and Finnish.

03

Interactive Pages

Dedicated sections for the restaurant, arcade machines, events and visitor information.
